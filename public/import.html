Import questions

Upload a CSV or JSON file. Nothing is saved until you have seen the check and pressed Save. Your file is copied. The original is not changed.

Columns

  • question, content (anatomy or physiology), dok (1, 2 or 3)
  • anat (gross or histology) for anatomy cards
  • Multiple choice: optionA to optionD and correct (A to D)
  • Image identification: image (file name in data/images) and answer (other spellings separated by |)
  • Optional: id, territory, topic, competency, explanation

Recall cards with no answer choices are not turned into multiple choice. They are listed for you to finish.
